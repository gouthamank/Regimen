Regimen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

What the app collects

  • Google Account email address — used only to identify your account for sign-in. Regimen does not request access to your contacts, Gmail, files, or any other Google data beyond your basic profile (email and name).
  • Workout data you enter in the app — routines, workout sessions, sets/reps/weight, cardio entries, body measurements, and app preferences (units, theme). This is the same data the app stores locally on your device; it is copied to the cloud only so it can sync across your own devices.

How it's used

Your data is used solely to back up and sync your own workout data across your own devices signed in with the same Google Account. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than making the app work for you.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), scoped so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it. No other user of the app can see your data, and the developer does not access it except as needed to fix bugs.

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared with any third party. It is not used for ads or analytics.

Deleting your data

You can delete your synced cloud data at any time from the app's Account screen, or by revoking Regimen's access from your Google Account permissions page. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally on your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer directly.
